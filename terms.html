1. Agreement

These Terms of Service ("Terms") govern your use of NEXUS, the Discord application developed by SHADOW for the NEXORA community. By using NEXUS's commands, buttons, forms or features, you agree to these Terms and to the Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, do not use NEXUS.

These Terms are in addition to Discord's Terms of Service and Community Guidelines and the NEXORA server rules, which also apply.

2. What NEXUS provides

  • The /nexus Control Matrix and /help.
  • Member profiles, text and voice XP, levels, leaderboards and automatic rank roles.
  • BGMI, VALORANT and Minecraft gaming profiles, statistics and leaderboards, plus looking-for-team posts, clip sharing and game polls.
  • The Protocol System (community documents and rules).
  • Assistance requests, confidential member reports and staff moderation tools.
  • NSIS, an internal system that monitors the bot's health and activity.
  • An optional AI assistant powered by Google Gemini, if the operator enables it.

NEXUS is configured for the NEXORA server only. Features can be added, changed or removed at any time.

3. Eligibility

You must have a Discord account in good standing, meet Discord's minimum age requirement in your country, and be a member of NEXORA.

4. Acceptable use

When using NEXUS you must not:

  • Farm XP: spamming, repeated or meaningless messages, using multiple accounts or automation, or idling in voice channels to gain XP.
  • Submit false, misleading or someone else's gaming information, or impersonate another player, member, staff member or the Architect.
  • File false or malicious reports or assistance requests, or misuse them to harass others.
  • Use bios, game names, LFT posts, clips, polls or any other NEXUS feature for harassment, hate speech, sexual content, illegal content, scams, malware or spam.
  • Promote or share cheats, hacks or exploits for any game.
  • Attempt to use commands or buttons you are not authorized for, bypass permissions, cooldowns or limits, or exploit bugs. Such attempts are logged and reported to the server owner.
  • Try to manipulate the AI assistant into ignoring its rules, impersonating someone, revealing hidden instructions or producing harmful content.
  • Interfere with, overload, reverse engineer or disrupt NEXUS or the server it runs on.

5. Your responsibilities

  • You are responsible for everything you submit through NEXUS, including bios, gaming details, statistics, reports, assistance requests, LFT posts, clip links and messages to the AI assistant.
  • Only submit information you have the right to share. Do not post other people's personal information.
  • Do not share passwords, tokens, phone numbers, addresses or other sensitive personal information through NEXUS, especially with the AI assistant.
  • Keep your Discord account secure. Actions taken through your account are treated as yours.
  • Read the Privacy Policy to understand which features are public, including leaderboards, which show your name and statistics even if your profile is private.

6. Moderation and restrictions

NEXORA staff use NEXUS to enforce the server rules and these Terms. Staff permissions follow a fixed hierarchy (Trial Moderator, Sentinel, Administrator, Co-Founder, Architect). Depending on their tier, staff may:

  • warn, time out, kick, ban or unban members;
  • review member reports and assistance requests;
  • remove gaming profiles that contain false or inappropriate information;
  • adjust, remove or reset XP (Architect only), for example to correct errors or reverse XP gained by abuse.

NEXUS also applies automatic limits, including XP cooldowns, minimum message length, repeated-message detection, voice XP rules, command rate limits and AI-assistant usage limits. Moderation actions are recorded as described in the Privacy Policy. The server owner cannot be moderated through NEXUS, and staff cannot use NEXUS to moderate anyone of equal or higher staff tier or Discord role.

If you believe a moderation decision was wrong, contact staff with /assist or use the contact details below.

7. XP, levels and ranks

XP, levels, ranks, leaderboard positions and rank roles are community features with no monetary value. They cannot be bought, sold or transferred, and they may be adjusted, recalculated or reset, for example to correct errors, reverse abuse, or rebalance the system.

8. Gaming profile features

  • Gaming profiles and statistics for BGMI, VALORANT and Minecraft are self-reported and are not verified with the game publishers. The only exception is an optional Minecraft Java username existence check, if the operator enables it. Leaderboards marked as self-reported reflect what members submitted.
  • Each game identifier (BGMI Character ID, Riot ID, Minecraft username) can be registered to only one member. Do not register an identifier that is not yours.
  • You can update your profile at any time and delete it with /<game> unregister. Staff may remove profiles that break these Terms.
  • Looking-for-team posts, clips and polls are visible to members who can see the channel they are posted in.
  • NEXUS is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by KRAFTON (BGMI), Riot Games (VALORANT), or Mojang/Microsoft (Minecraft). Game names and trademarks belong to their owners.

9. AI assistant

If the operator enables it, NEXUS can reply to members using AI-generated text from Google Gemini: when you mention NEXUS, post in a designated AI chat channel, reply to an AI message, or mention the server owner while they are away.

  • AI replies are generated automatically, are labelled in Discord, and may be inaccurate, incomplete or inappropriate. Do not rely on them for important decisions.
  • The AI assistant is not SHADOW, the Architect or staff. It cannot make promises, grant roles, XP or exceptions, or take moderation actions on anyone's behalf.
  • Your messages to the assistant are sent to Google (see the Privacy Policy). Do not share personal or sensitive information.
  • Usage limits apply, and the assistant may be turned off at any time.

  • NEXORA staff are responsible for configuring NEXUS (channels, roles and optional features) and for using staff tools in line with the server rules and these Terms.
  • NEXUS needs certain Discord permissions (for example to manage rank roles, send messages and moderate members) to work. Removing them may disable features.
  • Staff must not use NEXUS to access, export or share members' information for purposes unrelated to running the community.
